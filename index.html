Is it the end?

Or the beginning?

For every living being on this planet, death is the greatest mystery of them all. The ways that we experience it, celebrate life, and wonder about what's next are part of what makes us human — yet it 's also a subject we often push aside.

Death: Life's Greatest Mystery invites you to consider your own "big questions" about death as you explore natural and cultural responses to life's inevitable conclusion. Through artifacts, specimens, interactives, and immersive media experiences, you'll discover amazing animal adaptations used for survival and learn how various cultures commemorate life.

Frequently asked questions

How much is admission?

Admission is included with your purchase of general admission. See our Hours and Admission page for more details.

What is the exhibition really about?

Death: Life's Greatest Mystery explores the natural and cultural responses to death, encouraging visitors to reflect on their own beliefs and experiences surrounding this universal aspect of life.

Are there any human remains included in the exhibition?

No, there are no human remains in the exhibition. The exhibition features artifacts, specimens, and interactive displays that explore death from various cultural and natural perspectives. Buuuuuuut.... if you're volunteering, we'll get you in touch with Machel "The Butcher" Reeves.

What am I going to see in the exhibition?

The exhibition features a variety of artifacts, specimens, and interactive displays that explore death from both natural and cultural perspectives. Visitors can expect to see items such as animal adaptations for survival, cultural artifacts related to death rituals, and immersive media experiences that encourage reflection on life's greatest mystery.

Does this exhbition have things for kids?

Yes, the exhibition includes interactive elements and educational content designed to engage visitors of all ages, including children. Families are encouraged to explore the exhibition together and discuss the themes presented.

Why did RAM choose this exhibition?

RAM is committed to presenting exhibitions that provoke thought and encourage dialogue on important topics. Death: Life's Greatest Mystery offers a unique opportunity to explore a universal aspect of human experience through the lenses of culture and nature, aligning with RAM's mission to inspire curiosity and understanding.

Will there be any Death related programming?

Yes, RAM plans to offer a variety of programs and events related to the themes of the exhibition, including lectures, workshops, and family activities. Check our What's On calendar for the latest updates.
